# ComplySmart v1.1 — Evidence-Backed Compliance Intelligence

**MBA research prototype for fashion and textile supply-chain compliance harmonisation**

**Live prototype:** https://complysmart-ai.netlify.app/

---

## Overview

ComplySmart v1.1 is an interactive research prototype developed as the practical translation of an MBA Final Major Project examining:

- Audit Duplication
- Compliance Complexity
- Audit Fatigue
- Perceived Usefulness
- Intention to Adopt integrated compliance technology

The concept focuses on a key operational problem in fashion and textile supply chains: suppliers often prepare, submit and verify substantially overlapping information across multiple buyers, standards, certification schemes and assurance systems.

ComplySmart demonstrates how an integrated digital platform could make overlap visible, support controlled evidence reuse, improve audit readiness, strengthen corrective-action management and reduce unnecessary administrative repetition.

---

## Strategic Purpose

### Vision
Create a more connected compliance environment in which verified evidence is traceable, reusable where appropriate and easier to manage across multiple assurance systems.

### Mission
Make overlapping requirements visible without falsely treating different standards as equivalent, allowing compliance teams to focus on genuine differences, risk and improvement.

### Purpose
Reduce administrative friction created by repeated compliance activity and translate research findings into practical tools for requirement harmonisation, evidence reuse, audit readiness and CAPA management.

---

## Evidence-Backed Scope

The Requirement Harmonisation and Overlap Intelligence section is based on a requirement-level research matrix covering:

- **64 requirement/control families**
- **19 audit, assessment, certification, management-system and security programs**

The mapping covers:

- Governance and management
- Social and labour compliance
- Occupational health and safety
- Environmental management
- Chemical management
- Quality and technical controls
- Chain of custody and materials
- Supply-chain security

### Overlap scoring

The research matrix uses:

- **2 — Strong / direct overlap**  
  The same underlying factory control or evidence is clearly relevant across mapped programs.

- **1 — Partial / applicability-dependent overlap**  
  Related evidence may be reusable, but scope, depth, applicability or verification requirements differ.

- **0 — Not mapped**  
  The requirement was not established as a core overlap area from the public source used in the research.

---

## Important Research Boundary

**Overlap does not mean equivalence, mutual recognition or audit replacement.**

Different standards may assess the same underlying factory control or evidence while still differing in:

- clause wording
- thresholds
- sampling rules
- auditor methodology
- scoring
- governance requirements
- corrective-action procedures
- acceptance criteria

The prototype therefore focuses on **identifying avoidable repetition and potential evidence reuse**, not on claiming that different audits can automatically replace one another.

## Research-to-Prototype Logic

The prototype was designed around the study's core conceptual logic:

**Audit Duplication + Compliance Complexity → Audit Fatigue → Perceived Usefulness → Intention to Adopt**

The interface translates these constructs into practical features such as:

- requirement cross-mapping
- reusable evidence records
- audit-readiness visibility
- centralized compliance information
- CAPA tracking
- supplier-level compliance oversight
- identification of duplication hotspots

---

## Data and Confidentiality Boundary

This repository contains the **derived requirement-level mapping and prototype interface only**.

It intentionally excludes:

- confidential factory audit reports
- client-specific findings
- proprietary audit checklists
- supplier-identifiable confidential information
- commercially sensitive audit evidence

Facility-specific audit materials used during the research process were used only to identify recurring requirement patterns and are not reproduced in this repository.

---

## Prototype Status

ComplySmart v1.1 is an **illustrative research prototype**, not a validated commercial software product.

Operational elements shown in the interface, including:

- supplier profiles
- readiness percentages
- audit dates
- CAPA counts
- performance indicators
- estimated operational benefits

should be interpreted as **illustrative UI data unless explicitly identified as research-derived mapping results**.
