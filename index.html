Strategic foundation

Vision, mission and purpose

The concept is designed around a managerial problem: suppliers repeatedly prepare, submit and verify overlapping information across multiple assurance systems, while standards can still differ materially in thresholds and verification rules.

01 · VISION

Connected assurance

A fashion supply chain where verified evidence is traceable, reusable where appropriate and easier to manage across multiple customer and standard requirements.

02 · MISSION

Make overlap visible

Map common requirements and evidence without falsely treating standards as equivalent, so compliance teams can focus effort where differences and risk actually matter.

03 · PURPOSE

Reduce administrative friction

Translate the study’s audit-duplication and compliance-complexity findings into practical tools for requirement harmonisation, evidence reuse, audit readiness and corrective action.

Sustainable supply-chain governance

Better coordination, not “fewer audits at any cost”.

The concept supports governance by showing where evidence can be reused, where assurance remains program-specific and where corrective action requires management attention. It does not claim that one audit can automatically replace another.

TRACEABILITY

Controlled evidence reuse

Link common evidence to multiple compatible requirements while retaining source, scope, validity and program-specific review.

FOCUS

Hotspot intelligence

Use weighted overlap scores to identify administrative duplication hotspots and target harmonisation effort.

ASSURANCE

Preserve differences

Keep thresholds, sampling, auditor requirements, corrective-action rules and acceptance decisions visible rather than flattening them into false equivalence.
